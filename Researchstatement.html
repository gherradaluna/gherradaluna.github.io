Research Statement

Inspired by the story of my father, my research agenda focuses on where the fields of nutrition, cardiovascular health, and public health intersect. Through this research, consequently, I hope to explore the causal mechanisms, if any, between NOVA group 4 foods and blood pressure trajectories. Following this, I hope to investigate how or in what ways the findings of this research could be applied to rural communities, particularly those in Western North Carolina who struggle with lack of access to physicians. Looking ahead, I hope to translate these scientific findings into preventative strategies that lower hypertension rates among medically underserved communities.
